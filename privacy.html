Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

LikeSpot (https://ebru-tumer.github.io/likespot/) is a free, non-commercial song guessing game. It plays very short clips of songs you have liked and you guess the title. This policy explains what data LikeSpot accesses and what it does with it.

Data we access

  • YouTube (Google): if you choose "Connect with YouTube Music", LikeSpot asks for the read-only scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly and reads only the list of videos you have liked: video title, channel name, thumbnail, duration and video ID. It does not read your email, contacts, comments, subscriptions or any other account data, and it never modifies anything in your account.
  • Spotify: if you choose "Connect with Spotify", LikeSpot reads your Liked Songs (track name, artists, album, release year, cover image) and, only if you pick Spotify playback, controls playback in the browser tab.
  • Hit songs and game music modes: no sign-in; song lists and previews come from Deezer's and Apple's public APIs.
  • Public YouTube playlists: if you paste a playlist link, LikeSpot reads that public playlist without signing in.

How we use the data

The data is used only to build the guessing game for you: choosing which song to play, showing title suggestions while you type and revealing the answer. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Where the data is stored

LikeSpot has no server and no database. All data, including sign-in tokens, stays in your own browser (local storage). The site owner cannot see your songs, account or scores.

Sharing

LikeSpot does not sell, rent or share your data with anyone. To play a clip, the song title and artist name are sent to Apple's public iTunes Search API to find a 30-second preview, and what you type in the guess box is sent to Deezer's public search API to suggest song names; no account information is sent.

Google API Services User Data Policy

LikeSpot's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data and revoking access

  • Click Çıkış (Sign out) in LikeSpot to delete your song list and tokens from the browser, or clear the site data in your browser.
  • Revoke Google access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Revoke Spotify access at spotify.com/account/apps.
